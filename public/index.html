Before you start

Stillpoint uses your camera on this machine only. The SmartSpectra SDK reads your pulse, breathing, HRV and expressions from the camera feed.

  • Video is processed locally by the SDK on this machine. No video is stored, uploaded or shared.
  • Telemetry is off. No account, no login, no tracking.
  • Numbers are kept in your browser only if you press Save after a session. Press Delete to remove them.
  • These readings are for general wellness only. They are not a medical diagnosis, and they do not diagnose or treat anything.
  • The camera starts only when you press Start, and stops when you press Stop or the session ends.

For the best reading: face and upper chest visible, light on your face from the front, sit still, and do not talk during the session.

Timing: pulse takes about 12 seconds, breathing about 30 seconds, HRV about 60 seconds. That is why a session is 60 seconds.

Live mode needs a camera and a Presage API key on the server. If no key is set, the server runs in demo mode.

How the score is made, in plain words

Breathing is worth up to 40 points. If your breathing slowed, you score above 20. If it stayed the same, you score 20. If it got faster, you score below 20. Pulse is worth up to 30 points, in the same way. Stress index is worth up to 20 points, or 10 if HRV was not ready yet. A smile adds up to 10 points. Talking or moving a lot takes points away, up to 10. Nothing here is a medical score. It only compares your end with your own start.
